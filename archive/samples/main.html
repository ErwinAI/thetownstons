The Townstons is a Fansite wiki. Here you can find information and opinions from lots of players of the popular game Dungeon Runners! You can start increasing your knowledge by clicking on one of the categories below or on the menu to the left. They all contain valuable information which has been contributed by players like YOU!

  • Here is the FAQ which explains all about how this Wiki works.
  • Here is the Crew Page which lists the moderators.
  • The MOST important people in this Wiki are YOU, the players and contributors!


  • Dungeons: Schematic maps, outlines and details of all dungeons
  • Guides: Useful information including Interface Guide, Boss Guides, Tips and other stuff.
  • Skills: Information on the effects and costs of all skills.
  • Monsters: Descriptions, often pictures, and sometimes tips for all monsters
  • Items: Weapons, Armor, Jewelry and their uses, with a special section on Mythic (Rainbow) Items.
  • Quests: The Quests that make the game interesting.
  • NPC's: Non Player Characters and Objects you will encounter.
  • Towns/Outposts: The places in the game that are NOT dungeons!
  • Builds: Share experience on how to create the perfect character.
  • Game Mechanics: Those all important formulae and calculations, and a List of Equations.
  • Guilds (Posses): Groups of players working together cooperatively.
  • Glossary: Definitions of game terms, many with links to more extensive articles.


Players like YOU make this wiki successful!

Register: To be able to edit pages and have access to more pages which are unviewable for anonymous users you can register Here!

Editing a Page: If you are reading a page and have something useful to add, or a correction to make, look for the EDIT tab at the top of the page. Click it, and you will be able to make your additions or changes.

Creating a new page: See Page Entry Guides for easy entry templates for pages on Quests, Monsters, NPC's, Guilds and Rainbow Items. Or you can simply type in the name of the article you wish to create and click the "Create Article" button. If the article doesn't exist, it will immediately put you into the editor.


Getting Started: For people that are new with contributing at a wiki, there is a small guide that will explain everything about the "Code" of wiki pages (Text make up, pasting an image etc.) Beginner's Guide Click here!. If you don't want to do the coding, just type your article in plain text, and one of the mods will check it and may add the links and frills to it.
